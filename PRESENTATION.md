# 🌟 PRESENTATION — EXTASIS MARIMBA

**Extasis Marimba** is a **100% free and open-source (GPLv3)** virtual synthesizer built on custom Physical-FM modeling, designed to recreate the profound realism, acoustic depth, and percussive soul of the traditional Mexican and Mesoamerican marimba.

### Key Features:
* **100% Free & Open Source (GPLv3):** No DRM, no demo timers, no serial keys—completely free for everyone and open to community contributions.
* **True Lock-Free Physical-FM Engine:** Custom real-time C++ mathematical synthesis engine with zero sample libraries and ultra-low CPU footprint.
* **1:4:10 Tuned Mode Carving:** Faithfully models the acoustic physics of hand-carved *Hormiguillo* hardwood bars with Nyquist-safe upper mode handling.
* **Dynamic Membrane Buzz (*La Cachimba*):** Reproduces the velocity-gated rattling of the traditional pig-intestine and beeswax membrane.
* **Equal-Power Acoustic Key-Tracking:** Stereo panning (lows left, highs right) and physical decay curves (lows ring, highs snap) plus decorrelated stereo courtyard ambience.
* **Organic "Pueblo" Drift:** Deterministic per-key micro-imperfections and per-strike variations so every note feels alive.
* **Full MIDI Expressiveness:** 16-voice polyphony with smart voice stealing, Sustain Pedal (CC 64), and Pitch Bend (±2 semitones).
* **40 Categorized Presets + User Saving:** Covering Traditional Chiapaneca, Latin Club/Cumbia, Concert/World, and Experimental Hybrid FM, plus `INIT`, `RND` randomizer, and `.empreset` saving.
* **Refined 680×420 Studio GUI:** Symmetrical dark obsidian & warm amber-gold layout, double-click-to-reset knobs, interactive Logo Trigger Pad, and live LCD oscilloscope & 1:4:10 modal meters.
* **Cross-Platform Multi-Format:** VST3, AU, and Standalone for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
