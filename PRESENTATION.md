# 🌟 PRESENTATION — EXTASIS MARIMBA

**Extasis Marimba** is a specialized virtual synthesizer built on advanced physical-FM modeling, designed to recreate the profound realism, acoustic depth, and percussive nuances of the traditional Mexican and Mesoamerican marimbas. 

### Key Features:
* **True Physical-FM Engine:** Abandons heavy static sample libraries for a custom real-time C++ mathematical synthesis engine.
* **1:4:10 Tuned Mode Carving:** Perfectly mirrors the physics of a hand-carved hardwood bar.
* **Dynamic Membrane Buzz (La Cachimba):** Accurately reproduces the velocity-dependent rattling of pig intestine affixed with beeswax.
* **Acoustic Key-Tracking:** Intelligent stereo panning (Lows physical left, Highs physical right) and decay times (Lows ring, Highs snap) mapping the physical size of the instrument.
* **Organic "Pueblo" Drift:** Introduces raw, deterministic imperfections per key to ensure no two notes sound digitally sterile.
* **40 Categorized Presets:** Encompassing Traditional Chiapaneca, Concert Symphony, Latin Club/Cumbia, and Experimental Hybrid FM.
* **Sleek Custom GUI:** An elegant 660x410 dark obsidian interface with an interactive LCD oscilloscope and modal meters.
* **Multi-Format Support:** VST3, AU, and Standalone Application for macOS and Windows.
